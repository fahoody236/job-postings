BILINGUAL MARKETING WEBSITE — SAUDI ACCOUNTING SAAS

OVERVIEW

A public marketing website for a Saudi cloud accounting and finance SaaS platform serving Saudi SMEs. The product itself is already built — this project covers only the marketing site: explaining the product, presenting pricing, and driving trial sign-ups. No dashboards, no accounting functionality, no user accounts.

WHY THIS PRODUCT IS DIFFERENT

– Built for Saudi, not adapted for it: native ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing, VAT verified against regulation, Hijri + Gregorian calendar support
– Fully bilingual by design: Arabic and English throughout, true RTL layout, not a translated skin
– Honest by design: the system won't show a number it can't support
– AI-assisted, not AI-driven: AI helps categorize and explain, never writes to the accounts unattended
Target users: Saudi SME owners and bookkeepers, often working with an external accountant.
Competitors for reference (not imitation): Qoyod, Wafeq, Zoho Books, Xero.

SCOPE

Core pages: Home, Features, Pricing, About, Contact, Legal (Terms, Privacy, Refund Policy)
Optional, quote separately: Blog/resources, customer stories template, ZATCA compliance explainer, help documentation
REQUIREMENTS — BILINGUAL & RTL (the most important technical requirement)
– Two complete languages, every page, every element, no partial translation
– True RTL mirroring for Arabic: nav, layout, alignment, icons — not English layout with Arabic text dropped in
– Persistent language switcher on every page
– Proper Arabic web font (Cairo, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Tajawal, or similar)
– Independent SEO per language: correct hreflang, separate meta descriptions
– Western Arabic numerals, SAR currency

REQUIREMENTS — DESIGN

– Professional, modern, trustworthy; clarity over flourish
– Reference points: Xero, Stripe, Linear (international), Qoyod (local expectations)
– Mobile-first, majority of visitors expected on phones
– Fast: under 3 seconds on mobile
– Accessible: contrast, keyboard nav, alt text

REQUIREMENTS — TECHNICAL

– Platform open to recommendation (Webflow, Framer, or Next.js/Astro), must allow the client to edit copy without a developer
– Client provides domain; site at root, app at app. subdomain
– SSL, GA4 or Plausible analytics, Search Console for both languages with sitemap submitted
– Contact form with reliable delivery and spam protection
– Full SEO basics: meta titles/descriptions per page per language, Open Graph, semantic headings

PRICING PAGE SPECIFICS

– Flat monthly fee per company, SAR 90–400 range, storage limits with overage
– Comparison table, mobile-readable
– Annual/monthly toggle if applicable, clear storage-limit behavior, FAQ, prominent trial CTA

WHAT THE CLIENT PROVIDES

Product name and domain, logo/brand assets (or quote to create), product screenshots, final pricing/plans, legal page content, about page content, sign-up URL, contact details. Some items will arrive mid-project as the product nears launch — this is expected and should be reflected in your timeline.
Copywriting: state whether included and in which languages. Arabic copy must be written by a native speaker, not machine-translated.

DELIVERABLES

Complete live bilingual site
Full source/platform access transferred to client
Handover session or written content-editing guide
Analytics and Search Console configured and verified
Full testing across both languages, mobile and desktop
SEO checklist confirmed complete
PROPOSAL SHOULD INCLUDE
Recommended platform and reasoning, especially content editing
Approach to bilingual RTL, with examples if available
Timeline with milestones
Itemized cost: site, copywriting, brand work, optional pages, ongoing hosting
What's needed before starting vs. what can arrive mid-project
Relevant past work, especially bilingual Arabic/English sites

SUCCESS CRITERIA

– A Saudi business owner can land on the site, understand the product in 30 seconds, find pricing, and reach sign-up, on a phone, in Arabic
– Arabic reads naturally and the layout is correctly mirrored throughout
– Client staff can edit copy without a developer
– Both languages indexed by Google
– Mobile load time under 3 seconds
