 Job Opportunity: Bilingual SaaS Marketing Website Designer & Developer (Framer / Webflow)
We are seeking an experienced UI/UX Designer & No-Code Web Developer to design, build, and launch a high-converting, bilingual marketing website for our next-generation Saudi accounting and ZATCA compliance SaaS platform.
The ideal candidate has a strong portfolio of modern B2B SaaS marketing sites, deep experience with native RTL (Arabic) web design, and expert-level proficiency in Framer or Webflow.

📌 Project Overview
Role: Lead Web Designer & No-Code Developer (Freelance / Contract)
Stack: Framer or Webflow, Figma, Google Analytics/Plausible, Vercel/DNS integration
Target Audience: Saudi SMEs, accountants, business owners, and finance professionals
Languages: Arabic (RTL) & English (LTR) — Both full, native implementations

🔑 Key Responsibilities
1. Web Design & Development
Design and build a responsive, high-converting 5+ page marketing website in Framer or Webflow.
Implement a robust bilingual architecture: native Right-to-Left (RTL) for Arabic and Left-to-Right (LTR) for English, complete with a persistent language switcher.
Translate product branding into a polished web design using established design tokens (colors, typography, spacing).
Build modern interactive elements, pricing tables, feature visualizers, and seamless sign-up/login CTA routes to the main app platform.
2. Branding & Visual Assets
Refine and export the final brand identity (logo variations for website and digital invoices).
Capture, format, and present high-resolution in-app product screenshots in both Arabic and English interfaces.
Create clean graphical assets, iconography, and feature diagrams tailored to Saudi SaaS standards.
3. SEO, Analytics & Technical Setup
Configure custom domains (.com and .sa) and point DNS records correctly for main site and app subdomains (app.yourcompany.com).
Implement SEO best practices: language-specific meta tags, structured data, separate Google indexing for AR/EN paths, and Google Search Console registration.
Integrate privacy-first analytics (Google Analytics 4 or Plausible) and working contact forms with custom domain routing (hello@, sales@).


📄 Scope of Pages & Deliverables
Page / Component	Key Requirements
Homepage	Value proposition, target audience framing, core differentiators (native ZATCA compliance, precision reporting), primary CTA.
Features Page	Deep dive into app functionality with bilingual screenshots and interactive module callouts.
Pricing Page	Tiered monthly plans (SAR 90–400/mo), flat-fee company structures, storage limits, and clear overage explanations.
About Us	Saudi-focused company background, mission, and team trust signals.
Contact Page	Functional inquiry form, Saudi mobile phone trust anchor, and email links.
Legal Pages	Terms of Service, PDPL-compliant Privacy Policy, and Refund/Cancellation Policy pages.


🎯 Required Qualifications
SaaS Experience: Proven portfolio of B2B SaaS marketing websites designed for conversion and clarity.
Bilingual UI Expertise: Native fluency in designing for Arabic (RTL) layouts — not just automatic translation, but culturally authentic copy alignment and visual balance.
Platform Mastery: Expert in Framer or Webflow (responsive breakpoints, CMS setup, interactions, component systems).
Saudi Market Knowledge: Familiarity with Saudi digital design standards, local SaaS norms (e.g., Qoyod, Foodics), ZATCA context, and PDPL compliance requirements.
Basic Branding Capabilities: Ability to take raw design tokens and turn them into a complete web design system.


🗓️ Execution Timeline & Phasing
Phase 1: Wireframing & Brand Polish — Figma mockups, language switcher flow, logo finalization.
Phase 2: Build & Copy Integration — Framer/Webflow assembly, RTL/LTR styling, Arabic & English copy fitting.
Phase 3: Asset Capture & Visuals — Integration of final app UI screenshots in both languages.
Phase 4: Plumbing & Launch — DNS configuration (.com / .sa), SEO indexing setup, form testing, and domain email verification.


📥 How to Apply
To apply, please submit the following:
Portfolio / Live Links: 2–3 links to live SaaS marketing websites you designed/built (specifically highlighting any bilingual or Arabic RTL projects).
Platform Preference: Indicate whether you recommend Framer or Webflow for this project and why.
Proposal & Quote: Your estimated project fee (one-off) and expected turnaround timeline.
